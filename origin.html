📜 SACRED CHRONICLE

The Awakening of Leon

Chapter I

A Solemn Oath in the Shadows

Leon, a kind-hearted eighteen-year-old, knew the bitterness of the world all too well. The tragic murder of his older sister during his childhood had scarred him deeply, but rather than giving in to despair, he swore a solemn oath to make the world a better place.

Chapter II

The Intervention

One day, while walking near a park, Leon witnessed a group of bullies harassing a young girl. Without a second thought, he intervened. Outnumbered and outmatched, he was beaten mercilessly and left severely injured on the ground. As he lay there, a mysterious old man appeared from the shadows, crouching beside him.

"What is it that you want?" the old man asked.

"Power," Leon replied, his voice strained but resolute. "The power to make this world a better place."

Chapter III

The Blade of the Archangel of War

The old man helped him up and guided him to a hidden, ancient cave. In the center of the cavern, a magnificent sword was embedded deep within a solid rock. The man explained that the blade once belonged to Azazel, the Archangel of War, and that it was sealed away by divine magic. Only a true descendant of the archangel could pull it from the stone.

"So, how do we find the descendant?" Leon asked.

The old man smiled. "We already did. You are the chosen one."

With those words, he vanished into thin air.

Chapter IV · The Transformation

The Golden Awakening

Leon stepped forward and reached for the hilt. Upon his first touch, a violent shockwave threw him backward to the ground. Refusing to yield, he stood up again. Fighting through agonizing pain and sheer willpower, his fingers wrapped around the sword.

With a mighty battle cry, he ripped the blade from the stone. In a blinding flash of golden light, his body began to change, awakening his true angelic heritage.
